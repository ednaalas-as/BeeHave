BeeHave Pi setup

Connect your Pi to Wi-Fi. Use the setup code on its label. If the Pi was paired before, a farm owner can find it in the BeeHave app under Edge Device → Advanced settings → Setup label. This does not access or upload monitoring files.

Higher numbers are tried first when several saved networks are in range. Leave empty for 50.WPA2/WPA3 personal and open networks. Saved networks are kept; a failed attempt changes nothing.

What happens next

  1. In a few seconds your phone leaves this setup Wi-Fi while the Pi tries . That is expected.
  2. Wait about one minute, then look at your phone’s Wi-Fi list.
  3. The setup Wi-Fi is gone: the Pi is connected. Go back to your usual Wi-Fi and open BeeHave → Edge Device. The device shows the result of this Wi-Fi setup once it is back online (about 30 seconds).
  4. The setup Wi-Fi comes back: the Pi could not connect, and nothing was changed. Join the setup Wi-Fi again, reopen this page and enter the setup code: the reason is shown here.
